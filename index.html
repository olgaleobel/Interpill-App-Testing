About Interpill

Interpill provides personalised drug–drug interaction checks. Learn more →

Tap below to download the app for testing.

Step 1 – Download the app

Tap the button below to download the Interpill app to your Android device.

Download Interpill App

The file will appear in your Downloads folder.

Step 2 – Install the app
1. Open the downloaded file on your phone.
2. If a security message appears, choose Allow installation.
3. Once installed, find Interpill in your apps and open it.
Step 3 – Try the app

Once Interpill is installed, you can explore it freely or follow some suggested testing scenarios prepared for participants.
