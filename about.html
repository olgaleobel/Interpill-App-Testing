About Interpill

Background information for testers and supervisors.

Purpose of the app

Interpill is a Kotlin Multiplatform mobile application designed to support personalised drug–drug interaction checks. The aim is to give users a clearer view of how their medicines may interact, taking into account their individual health profile.

Key functionality

The current evaluation build of Interpill allows users to:

  • search for medicines and review structured interaction information,
  • scan medication packaging to look up selected medicines,
  • view key sections of official drug information (labels),
  • keep a simple medication schedule with quick interaction checks for all added medicines.

Personalisation

Interaction analysis is personalised using the user’s recorded allergies and medical conditions. These details are used to highlight interactions or warnings that may be more relevant for that specific user.

Data sources and AI support

Interpill combines information from recognised medicine data sources (such as RxNorm, OpenFDA and DailyMed) with an additional AI-generated summary. The AI component is used to provide a short, readable overview of potential interactions, while the underlying interaction logic is based on structured data and rules.

The AI summary is intended to complement, not replace, the underlying data. It is deliberately conservative and designed to be easy to understand.

Project context

Interpill has been developed as part of my final-year BSc Computer Science project at the University of West London. The current build is an evaluation version intended for research and usability testing.

Important notes for testers

Interpill is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It is a research prototype and must not be used to make real clinical decisions. Any concerns about medicines or interactions should always be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
