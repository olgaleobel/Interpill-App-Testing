Скопируй **весь код** в `emulator.html` (полностью заменить содержимое файла): ```html Interpill – Test in browser

Interpill – Testing without an Android phone

You can try Interpill in a web-based Android emulator if you do not have an Android device.

Before you start

The online emulator runs Interpill on a virtual Android phone in your browser. Some features, such as camera-based scanning of medication packaging, are not available in this mode.

Step 1 – Sign up or log in

Open the emulator provider’s site in a new tab and create a free account (or log in if you already have one).

Open emulator site (Sign up / Log in)

A free account normally provides a limited amount of testing time each month.

Step 2 – Open the Interpill session

After signing in, use the link below to start the Interpill emulator session in your browser.

Launch Interpill emulator

You may see a “Tap to play” button – click it to start the virtual device.

Step 3 – Test Interpill
1. Once the virtual phone has loaded, use Interpill as you would on a normal Android device: search for medicines, add them to checks, and review the interaction information.
2. If the session stops or times out, you can reopen the emulator link and start again while your free time is still available.

Camera-based scanning of medication packaging is not supported in the browser emulator. All other main interaction features are available for testing.

Step 4 – Submit your feedback

After testing Interpill in the emulator, please complete the feedback form:

Open feedback form

← Back to main download page
``` Если хочешь, потом можем чуть упростить текст (например, убрать упоминание про ограничение по минутам) или добавить короткий скриншот-гайд, но базовая версия с **Sign up / Log in** и кнопкой запуска эмулятора уже есть.
