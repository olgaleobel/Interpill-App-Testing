Interpill app – Testing with Online Emulator

If you do not have an Android phone, you can still try Interpill app using a free online emulator.

Before you start

In this option you will first download the Interpill app (APK file) to your computer, then upload it to a free online Android emulator (for example Appetize.io). The main interaction and information features are available, but scanning medication packaging is not supported in this mode.

Step 1 – Download the Interpill app (APK)

Download the Interpill APK file to your computer. You will use this file in the next step when uploading the app to the emulator website.

Download Interpill app (APK)

The APK file will normally appear in your Downloads folder unless you choose a different location.
Step 2 – Create a free emulator account and upload the APK

One option is to use Appetize.io, which offers a free tier with limited minutes. The exact interface may change slightly over time, but the basic steps are:

  1. Open https://appetize.io in your browser.
  2. Select Sign up and create a free account, or Log in if you already have one.
  3. After signing in, choose the option to upload a new app (for example Upload or Add new app).
  4. When prompted, select the Interpill APK file you downloaded in Step 1 and wait for the upload to finish.
Once the upload is complete, Interpill should appear as an app entry in your emulator dashboard.
Step 3 – Try Interpill app in the emulator

From your emulator dashboard, start the Interpill app (for example by clicking its card and choosing Launch).

  1. When the virtual phone appears, wait until Interpill app finishes loading.
  2. You can explore the app freely or follow some suggested testing scenarios.

Open example testing scenarios →

Emulator sessions may be time-limited. If the screen resets or stops responding, you can usually refresh the page or start a new session from your emulator dashboard. The scanning feature requires a real camera and is therefore not available in the emulator.
