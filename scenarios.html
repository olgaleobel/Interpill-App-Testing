Interpill – Example testing scenarios

Optional prompts to help you explore the app during testing.

You may use Interpill in any way that feels natural to you. The scenarios below are suggestions that can help you try different parts of the app. You do not need to complete all of them.

Scenario 1 – Checking your usual medicines

  1. Add two or three medicines that you take regularly to the list.
  2. Run an interaction check for these medicines.
  3. Read the interaction summary and any highlighted notes.
  4. Consider whether the information is clear and easy to understand.

Scenario 2 – Adding a new medicine

  1. Imagine that your doctor has added a new medicine to your current treatment.
  2. Add this new medicine to the list alongside the existing ones.
  3. Run an interaction check again and compare the results.
  4. Note whether it is obvious what has changed and how it is shown on screen.

Scenario 3 – Using the schedule

  1. Open the medication schedule and add at least two medicines with different times.
  2. Check how easy it is to review what you are taking in a single day.
  3. Use the quick interaction check from the schedule view, if available.

Scenario 4 – Looking up information about one medicine

  1. Search for a single medicine in the app.
  2. Open its information page and scroll through the main sections (for example indications, warnings, interactions).
  3. Decide whether the layout and wording help you to find the information you would expect.

Please remember that Interpill is a research prototype and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Do not change any real treatment on the basis of this app.
