Interpill – Test in your browser

If you do not have an Android phone, you can run Interpill in an online Android emulator.

Step 1 – Open the emulator

Click the button below to open Interpill in your web browser.

Open Interpill in browser

A new tab will open with a virtual Android phone. A stable internet connection is recommended.

Step 2 – Use the virtual phone
1. When the virtual phone appears, tap the screen or the Tap to play button to start the session.
2. Once Interpill loads, use it as you would on a normal Android device: search for medicines, add them to checks, and review the interaction information.
3. If the session stops or times out, you can reopen the link and start again.

Please note that camera-based scanning of medication packaging is not available in the browser emulator.

Step 3 – Submit your feedback

After finishing your testing session, please complete the feedback form:
