Interpill app – Testing with Online Emulator

If you do not have an Android phone, you can still try Interpill app using a free online emulator.

Before you start

The online emulator runs Interpill app inside your browser for up to around 30 minutes of use. The main interaction and information features are available, but scanning medication packaging is not supported in this mode.

Step 1 – Open the emulator

Click the button below to open Interpill app in the online Android emulator.

Open Interpill app in online emulator

If the emulator opens in a new tab and you see a loading screen, please wait a few seconds until the virtual device appears.
Step 2 – Create an account or sign in

The emulator website may ask you to sign in or create a free account before starting the session.

  1. Choose Sign up or Log in on the emulator website.
  2. You can usually use a Google account or an email address with a password.
  3. After signing in, return to the Interpill emulator page if needed.
Step 3 – Try Interpill app in the emulator

When the virtual phone appears, wait until Interpill app finishes loading.

Once Interpill app is ready, you can explore it freely or follow some suggested testing scenarios prepared for participants.

Open example testing scenarios →

Emulator sessions may be time-limited. If the screen resets or stops responding, you can simply refresh the page to start a new session. The scanning feature requires a real camera and is therefore not available in the emulator.
Step 4 – Share your feedback

After you have finished exploring Interpill app in the emulator, please complete the main feedback form:

Open feedback form

This page is intended for study and evaluation purposes. If you encounter any technical issues with the emulator, you can also describe them in the feedback form.
