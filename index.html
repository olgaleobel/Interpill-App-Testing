Interpill. Testing and Feedback

Interpill app provides personalised drug–drug interaction checks.

Step 1 – Download and Install
Download Interpill App

Step 2 – Try the app

Explore it freely! Or follow some suggested testing scenarios:

Scenario 1 – Checking two medicines together
  • Run an interaction check for Caffeine and Ibuprofen.
  • Review the result and any highlighted warnings.
Scenario 2 – Allergy-related risk
  • Imagine a person with an allergy to fish or fish oil (first add this allergy to the profile).
  • Find a product containing Omega-3 acids and check its information using Drug Check.
  • See whether the app highlights anything relevant to the allergy.
